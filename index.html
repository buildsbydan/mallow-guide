01 / Arrival & parking

We want your arrival to be simple. Your gate and door codes are sent privately through Airbnb on your arrival day and stay active until checkout.

  • Check-in: 3:00 PM or later
  • Checkout: By 11:00 AM
  • Parking: Please park only within the gated driveway.
  • Gate: Enter your gate code on the keypad at the gate.
  • Front door: Enter your door code on the front door keypad, then press the check mark button to unlock.
  • Leaving by car: A gate remote hangs on the key holder near the front door, inside the house. Point it toward the gate and press any button. Please return it to the key holder afterward. To come back, use your gate code.

For security, gate and door codes are not shown on this page. Look for them in your Airbnb messages.

02 / Wi-Fi & work

Wi-Fi is provided, and the dedicated workspace includes four monitors, ideal for remote work or longer stays.

  • Wi-Fi network: THE MALLOW
  • Password: Included in your Airbnb check-in details.

If you have trouble connecting a device to the monitors, message us and we'll help.

03 / House rules

Please treat the home with care and be considerate of our neighbors.

  • Registered guests: Maximum of six overnight guests.
  • Smoking: No smoking or vaping.
  • Parties / events: Parties, large gatherings and events are not permitted.
  • Quiet hours: 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM.
  • Pets: No pets. Service animals are welcome.
  • Neighbors: Please keep noise down and respect the surrounding community.

The house rules on the Airbnb listing govern if any details here differ.

04 / Around the home

  • Air conditioning and heating: The thermostat is in the second-floor hallway. Adjust it to your comfort.
  • TV and streaming: TVs are provided throughout the home. You can sign in to your own streaming accounts. Please sign out before you leave.
  • Laundry: The washer and dryer are in the second-floor hallway and are yours to use during your stay.
  • Trash and recycling: When the indoor trash can is full, take extra trash to the black bin outside, near the front gate.
  • Kitchen: Please wash used dishes and leave appliances switched off after use.
  • Tesla charging: A Level 1 EV charger is available on the left side of the building when facing the front door. Please handle the equipment carefully and return it to its place after use.

05 / Safety & privacy

  • Cameras: Exterior security cameras monitor the front, sides and rear of the property, including the driveway, gate and entrance. There are no cameras inside the home.
  • Smoke alarms: Installed throughout the property.
  • Fire extinguisher: Located under the kitchen sink.
  • Emergencies: Call 911 immediately.

06 / Checkout

We hope you had a comfortable stay. Before leaving by 11:00 AM, please:

  • Turn off lights, TVs and appliances.
  • Place any extra trash in the black bin near the front gate.
  • Sign out of your personal streaming accounts.
  • Return the gate remote to the key holder near the front door, inside the house.
  • Check for your belongings and chargers, and make sure all doors are closed and locked.

That's it! We'll take care of the rest.

07 / Need anything?

For non-emergency help, please message us through your Airbnb reservation. We'll do our best to respond promptly.

Emergencies: Call 911 for police, fire or medical emergencies.
